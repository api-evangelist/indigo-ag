The collaboration helps rice farmers adopt regenerative farming practices and improve water stewardship CHICAGO, IL - Nov. 24, 2025 -- Kellanova, Walmart and Indigo Ag today announced a new partnership designed to strengthen farmer prosperity through regenerative agriculture across Arkansas. Through the Source by Indigo program, the collaboration provides farmers with resources, data, and technical support to build more resilient and profitable rice farming operations across the state.
