Flagship Pioneering announced the formation of Terion, a new agricultural technology company that combines CIBO Technologies and Indigo Ag's Source business to unify fragmented agricultural data, verification, and programs into a trusted, AI-enabled platform serving farmers, enterprises, and governments. Led by CEO Sunand Menon (former CIBO Technologies CEO) and headquartered in Boston with operations in the US Midwest and Brazil, Terion aims to be the digital backbone for modern agriculture, connecting on-farm activity to enterprise sustainability, compliance, financing, and market outcomes.
