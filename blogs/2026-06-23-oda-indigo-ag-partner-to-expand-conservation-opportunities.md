COLUMBUS, Ohio. — June 23, 2026 — Ohio De partment of Agriculture (ODA) Director Brian Baldridge and Indigo Ag announced a new public-private partnership that will provide additional financial incentives to Ohio farmers implementing conservation practices through H2Ohio , Governor Mike DeWine’s statewide water quality initiative.
